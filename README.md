# Sales & Profit Performance Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

An interactive, three-page Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales and profitability performance across different countries, customer segments, product categories and sales channels from 2022 to 2025.

This project transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights, helping stakeholders understand revenue trends, identify profitable products and markets, evaluate customer performance, and uncover opportunities for business growth.

## Business Objectives

The analysis aims to answer the following business questions:

* How is overall sales and profit performance changing over time?
* Which countries contribute the most to total revenue?
* Which products generate the highest sales and profit?
* Which customer segments contribute most to profitability?
* Which sales channels perform best?
* What seasonal patterns and opportunities can be identified?

## Key Business Insights

The analysis revealed several important findings:

1. **Strong Performance in 2025:** Sales increased from $1.91M to $2.30M, representing approximately 20% growth after relatively flat performance between 2022 and 2024.

2. **Seasonal Sales Patterns:** Sales and profit performance peak between October and December, highlighting a strong fourth-quarter trend.

3. **Geographical Revenue Concentration:** The United States contributes approximately 43% of total sales, while the top three countries account for 73% of revenue.

4. **Product Revenue Concentration:** AeroBook 14 and ProBook 16 account for approximately 33% of total sales, while the top 10 products contribute 75%.

5. **Sales Channel Performance:** Online sales lead with approximately $3.1M, while phone orders record the lowest contribution at $0.6M.

6. **Customer Segment Profitability:** The Consumer segment contributes approximately $0.76M of the $1.65M total profit.

7. **Revenue Does Not Always Equal Profitability:** Although laptops dominate sales, products such as the Standing Desk and Vision 27 Monitor generate higher profits, demonstrating the importance of evaluating profitability alongside revenue.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the findings, the following recommendations were identified:

* **Diversify Product Revenue:** Reduce dependence on the two leading laptop products by promoting other products with growth potential.
* **Review Product Profitability:** Investigate the pricing and cost structures of high-revenue laptop products to identify opportunities for improving profit margins.
* **Prepare for Seasonal Demand:** Align inventory, staffing and marketing campaigns with the October–December sales peak.
* **Expand Market Opportunities:** Explore growth opportunities in smaller markets such as France and Australia.
* **Strengthen Sales Channels:** Investigate opportunities to improve B2B and phone sales performance.
* **Retain Valuable Customers:** Maintain strong relationships with Consumer customers while developing strategies to grow the Small Business and Corporate segments.
